# ClipStack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

ClipStack does not collect, send or share any of your data.

- The history of what you copy is saved only on your Mac, inside ClipStack's own folder.
- Nothing leaves your Mac. ClipStack does not connect to the internet.
- Items that password managers mark as private are never saved.
- You can clear the history at any time in ClipStack's Settings. Deleting the app removes it.
